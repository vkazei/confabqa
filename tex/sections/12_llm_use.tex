\section*{Use of Large Language Models}

The paper and code were developed with the author-guided assistance of
Anthropic's Claude Code (CC). I posed the research questions, designed
the ConfabQA benchmark and its prompt-feature control, and made the
modeling and interpretation decisions. Among tasks with required
disclosure, CC implemented the probing, intervention,
sparse-autoencoder, and statistical-analysis code and refined
experiments (for example, the random-direction controls and the
direct-optimization recovery); the interpretation of results was
developed in dialogue with CC. The ConfabQA question-and-answer items
were generated using Google Gemini in Deep Research mode, which also
performed the independent regrade of Section 4. Among tasks with
recommended disclosure, CC drafted the initial manuscript and co-edited
it, created figures, formatted the references, and searched for related
literature.

I reviewed the AI-assisted code, numbers, and citations, and I take
responsibility for the final content of this work.
